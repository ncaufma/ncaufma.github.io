The Artificial Intelligence & Data Science research line at NCA-UFMA develops intelligent computational systems capable of extracting insights from complex, high-volume, and heterogeneous data sources. Our work addresses real-world challenges across the energy sector, public administration, healthcare, and logistics.

Key research threads include customer dissatisfaction detection in electric utility data, legal judgment outcome prediction for consumer lawsuits using ensemble models, time series prediction and imputation for energy consumption, spatial clustering of residential consumers, and data-driven public management dashboards.
